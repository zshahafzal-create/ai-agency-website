Frequently Asked Questions

The essentials, clearly explained.

Will AI replace our customer service team?

The goal is to support your team with routine inquiries and better routing. Human review and escalation remain essential for complex, sensitive, or unusual situations.

Can this work with our current systems?

It depends on your communication channels, existing tools, permissions, and available integrations. We assess compatibility during discovery before recommending an implementation.

How do you handle customer information?

Data access, retention, permissions, and any third-party services must be agreed for each project. Sensitive workflows require appropriate safeguards and a clear human escalation policy.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on call or message volume, integrations, scope, and ongoing support. We discuss your requirements before proposing a tailored quote.
