TRANSPARENCY & TRUST

Privacy Policy.

An overview of what information may be collected when you visit our website, contact us, or book a consultation.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

ZeroWait AI (“we”, “our” or “us”) provides information about AI chatbots, voice agents, and business automation services through this website. We aim to be transparent about the information involved when visitors communicate with us.

2. Information you provide

When you request a consultation or communicate with us, you may choose to provide your name, business name, contact details, meeting preferences, and the information you share in messages or appointment notes. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, medical records, or other sensitive personal data through general inquiries.

3. Website and technical information

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting service and your browser may process technical information required to deliver the site, such as requests, device or browser information, and network identifiers. We do not currently operate a custom tracking or advertising script in the website code.

4. Appointment scheduling

Our consultation calendar is provided by Calendly. When you view or use that calendar, Calendly may process information according to its own policies and settings. We use booking information to arrange the meeting and follow up on your inquiry.

5. Live chat and AI-assisted conversations

If a live chat widget is enabled on this website, messages and contact details you submit through it may be processed by the chat platform and connected AI service to respond to your questions, route requests, and create useful conversation summaries. We will identify and review the active service providers before enabling such a widget on this site. AI-assisted responses may sometimes be incomplete or inaccurate; a human conversation can be requested when available.

6. How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide requested information.
  • To schedule and conduct introductory calls.
  • To understand business needs and discuss suitable services.
  • To maintain site security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable.

7. Sharing with service providers

Information may be processed by providers involved in website hosting, scheduling, and any live-chat functionality that is activated. We do not sell personal information through this website. Service providers may operate in other countries, and their processing practices are described in their respective policies.

8. Retention and security

We aim to retain inquiry and booking information only while reasonably needed for communication, service discussions, recordkeeping, or legal obligations. Actual retention also depends on the settings and policies of the services used. We take reasonable precautions, but no online service can promise absolute security.

9. Your choices and requests

You can decide what information to share in an inquiry. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information. To make a privacy-related request, contact ZeroWait AI through the consultation link and identify the nature of your request so we can arrange a suitable channel to respond.

10. Cookies and external services

Our own website code does not set an advertising cookie. Embedded third-party features such as Calendly, or a live chat widget if later activated, may use cookies or similar technology under their own controls. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

11. Updates to this policy

We may update this notice when our website, providers, or information practices change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

This notice describes the current website setup and should be reviewed whenever new integrations or customer-data workflows are added.
